This is a
list of Pokémon that are available in
Pokémon Champions
. 187 Pokémon species are available in the game, as well as 59
Mega Evolutions
.
Functionally distinct, permanent
forms
(such as
regional forms
) are listed alongside the base forms of their respective species, while other forms (such as
Mega Evolutions
) are listed in separate sections below. A Pokémon is considered "normally available" if it can be obtained without the use of events or
transferring
it from another game.
